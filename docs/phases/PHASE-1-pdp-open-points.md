# Phase 1: product page, open points to discuss

Written 2026-10-12 after the redesign (sub-phase 1j) was built and the owner's first round of changes was made. Nothing here is built.
It is the list to decide from: what the page used to have that it no longer has, what was never built, and what I would change to
make it look and feel better. Each row says what I would do, and the choice is yours.

How to read the "Suggest" column: **Leave** means I would not bring it back; **Bring back** means I would; **Your call** means it is a
real trade-off.

## 1. Cleaned up in this pass (done)

- Removed styles nothing used (`add`, `more`, `flipped` on the page; `tiles`, `tile`, `pillRow` on the skeleton).
- Removed messages nothing reads: `product.moreFrom`, `netQuantity`, `viewMore`, `viewLess` and the four `product.trust.*` tiles.
- Removed `moreFromShop` (and its three tests) and `CardTitle` (the heading-with-a-line the old cards used).
- Gate after the clean-up: lint, typecheck and format pass; 538 customer tests and 30 i18n tests pass.

## 4. Suggestions to improve the look and feel

Ordered by how much each would change what a shopper sees, with the cost.

| #   | Suggestion                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Why                                                                                                                                                  | Cost   |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| A   | **Say the delivery time once.** The header pill and the info card both say "10–15 mins". Keep the green pill and make the card's line "Packed today · Shelf life 2 days".                                                                                                   | Less repetition; the card line then carries the fact the pill does not, and the pill is stronger because it is the only one.                         | Small  |
| B   | **Group the three rows into one card with dividers** (Sold by, brand, replacement), or keep Sold by as a card and make the other two light lines inside the info card.                                                                                                      | Three separate boxes with gaps cost about 90 dp and read as three equal things; they are not equal.                                                  | Small  |
| C   | **One green, used on purpose.** The page now has the pistachio price badge, the green-tint pill, the green View details, "Packed today", "Also at N shops" and "Best value". Make the tinted pill one shared style and use it only for time, availability and View details. | Green is starting to mean everything. A short rule keeps the price badge the loudest green.                                                          | Small  |
| D   | **Put the brand above the name**, small and tappable ("Amul ›"), and the product name under it ("Taaza Toned Milk"). Then the brand row can go.                                                                                                                             | "Aashirvaad Wheat atta" in one line reads as a long name; two lines read as a brand and a product. It is also how the packs read. Saves a whole row. | Medium |
| E   | **Show price per unit on the "Other brands" cards** ("₹54/L").                                                                                                                                                                                                              | That is the reason to compare brands. Today the rail shows prices for different pack sizes side by side.                                             | Small  |
| F   | **Let the stage give back height**: ratio from 1.1 to about 1.25, or shrink the stage a little as the page scrolls.                                                                                                                                                         | The stage is 327 dp tall; the strip, the name and the size cards start lower than they need to. With real photos a squarer stage is fine.            | Small  |
| G   | **Real photos on a soft ground**: a transparent-background pack shot with a faint shadow under it, and the stage tint a little deeper in dark mode.                                                                                                                         | The emoji stand-ins look like stickers. Photos are the biggest single change to how the page feels.                                                  | Data   |
| H   | **Put "Savings" on each size card** ("Save ₹2") and make the best-value size the one that is pre-selected.                                                                                                                                                                  | The decision is made on the size cards; today they show a struck price and a unit price but not what you save.                                       | Small  |
| I   | **In the sheet, skip the chips the strip already showed**, and put the pack's price and size at the top.                                                                                                                                                                    | The first thing the sheet repeats is the strip.                                                                                                      | Small  |
| J   | **A small "Added" moment**: on ADD the button's colour pulses once and a light haptic fires (needs a package: ask first).                                                                                                                                                   | The press already shows; the result (it is in the cart) should too.                                                                                  | Small  |
| K   | **Order the strip by kind**: fresh goods lead with Shelf Life; packaged goods with Product Type; dairy with Fat Profile.                                                                                                                                                    | The first chip is the one most read.                                                                                                                 | Small  |
| L   | **Split `ProductView`** (about 800 lines) into the stage, the info card, the rows, the rails and the sheet, each with its own skeleton.                                                                                                                                     | The review recommended it; it keeps the page and its skeleton in step, and makes the next change safer.                                              | Medium |
