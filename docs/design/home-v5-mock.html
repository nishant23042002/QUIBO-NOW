Quibo Home, version 5

Version 5 rebuilds Know your shop around what a customer can check, and removes From your neighbours. Switch Returning and New customer to see where it sits.

Try

Trust, and where it shows up

  • Know your shop. One idea: a customer cannot check a dark store and can check a shop. A draggable scene opens it, five questions follow as swipeable cards, a 0 of 5 against 5 of 5 score sums it up, and an emotional close points to the shops. New customers meet it first, straight under the payment strip. Returning customers see it after Bestsellers. It names no company and says nothing about anyone's food.
  • The Quibo promise. Near the bottom, with a "How we check a shop" sheet. This is where a doubtful customer ends up.
  • Small repeats. A "Visited shops only" pill under the hero, the shop's name on the order card, and "Your money stays in Roha" in the footer. The "From your neighbours" section is removed.
  • Moment screens. One trust fact each, skippable with a tap, about 1.5 seconds. They appear on launch, on every third navigation, and when an order is placed. Never on every tap, never while loading, and they step aside with reduced motion. Tap View cart in the phone to see the order moment.

The four new pieces, and where they sit

  • Buy again. First module for a returning customer, above Fresh today. It is the shortest path to a full cart. One tap adds everything that is in stock, and out-of-stock items are named, not hidden. New customers never see it.
  • Language chip. In the header beside Profile, because that is where a person looks first. A new customer also gets a one-time card at the top of the page with the three languages. The sheet remembers the choice.
  • Pay on delivery. In a payment strip right under the hero, where Zepto and Blinkit put their payment offers. It is the only filled badge in the strip so a nervous first-time customer finds it. It should repeat at the cart and checkout.
  • Outside the area. Replaces Home instead of showing an empty one. It says why, offers Notify me, lists the towns you serve and lets the customer look around Roha anyway.

Categories, redone

  • Grouped like Zepto. Group title, a wide first tile, then four across. Live categories open; the rest are softened with a "Coming soon" tag.
  • Notify me. Tap a coming-soon tile or card and it turns green. Each tap is a signal of demand you can read before you onboard a shop.
  • Home shows two groups, then a "Coming soon to Roha" row, then All categories. The Categories tab (try it) shows every group and has a Find a category box.
  • Where it sits. After Fresh today, Bestsellers, Continue browsing, Daily essentials and Small prices, which are the fast paths. Only then does the full map appear, as Blinkit does. The header tabs already give one-tap entry to the top categories.

New in this version

  • Live order card. On top of Home while an order is active. A range, never a countdown.
  • Shops closed. The header says the next window, a banner explains, and adding still works for tomorrow.
  • Can't find it? Customers tell their shops what they need. Fits a town where the catalogue will always have gaps.
  • Why Quibo. Three trust points for first-time customers, and a help row in the footer.

LockedStill unchanged

  • Product card at 128 wide.
  • Bottom bar and cart bar, including free-delivery progress and the folding note.
